Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your profile, transactions, friendships and uploads go immediately; encrypted backups clear within 30 days. Details in the Privacy Policy.

How do payment requests and splits work?

Tag friends on an expense to split it evenly or by exact amounts. Both of you see who owes whom, and settling marks it paid on both sides.

How do I manage my iMoney Pro subscription?

iMoney is free to use; Pro is optional. Apple sells and bills it, so we never see your payment details. Manage it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → iMoney. Cancelling keeps iMoney Pro until the end of the paid period.

Does iMoney connect to my bank?

No. iMoney never links to your bank accounts or credit cards and never asks for those credentials — you type each entry yourself. It isn't a bank, a payment processor, or a money transmitter, and it never moves money: settling up happens between you and your friend, however you already do that.

What do you do with my data?

There are no ads in iMoney and no advertising SDKs. We don't sell your data or build advertising profiles. iMoney doesn't read your contacts or calendar, doesn't track your location, and doesn't follow you across other apps and websites. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What does signing in share?

iMoney uses Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google so there's no extra password to keep. Either one gives us your name, email address, and profile picture — nothing more. iMoney cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or Photos. Section 6 of the Privacy Policy spells out how to disconnect either one.

How do I turn notifications off?

iOS Settings → Notifications → iMoney. Everything works without them. You can also mute your own log pings per-friend inside the app.

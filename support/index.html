Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile, transactions, friendships, and uploads immediately; encrypted backups roll off within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do payment requests and splits work?

Tag friends on any expense to split it evenly or by exact amounts. Your friend sees their share, you both see who owes whom, and settling marks it paid on both sides.

How do I manage my iMoney Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → iMoney. Cancelling keeps iMoney Pro active until the end of the paid period.

How do I turn notifications off?

iOS Settings → Notifications → iMoney. The app works fully without them. You can also mute just your own log pings per-friend inside the app.
